# GreatPM product workflow

GreatPM organizes product work into a continuous six-stage loop:

1. **Discover** — establish the problem with customer and market evidence.
2. **Strategize** — choose the customer, position, advantage, and measurable
   outcome.
3. **Prioritize** — compare opportunities and make explicit tradeoffs.
4. **Define** — turn the selected opportunity into testable requirements,
   success metrics, risks, and non-goals.
5. **Launch** — prepare rollout, communications, support, observability, and
   rollback.
6. **Measure** — compare outcomes with the committed targets, capture learning,
   and feed the evidence into the next discovery cycle.

## Operating principles

- Start with evidence, not a predetermined feature.
- Keep assumptions visibly separate from verified facts.
- Use the smallest method that can answer the current decision.
- Make acceptance criteria and success thresholds testable.
- Preserve dissent, risks, and unresolved decisions in the artifact.
- Treat launch as the start of measurement, not the end of the workflow.
- Require a person to approve consequential strategy, scope, and launch
  decisions.

The remote MCP server can recommend methods and prepare this workflow. It does
not execute stages, access a project, contact other systems, or make approvals.
